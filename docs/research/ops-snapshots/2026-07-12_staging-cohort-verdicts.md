## 0. Data-quality notes (read these before the tables — they change how to read the numbers)

1. **`strategy_executions` decision-log volume is NOT trade count.** The live loop re-evaluates and re-logs a decision roughly every 30–60 seconds, far more often than the 1h candle. Over the cohort window this produced 1,326 `opened_short`/`opened_long` **decision-log rows** on staging — but only **4 real position events** happened (`positions`/`trades` tables, the ground truth). The identical artifact exists in **prod's** logs (1,017 `opened_*` rows in the same window against **zero** new prod trades). This is a system-wide logging characteristic, not a cohort-flag effect — worth a follow-up observability fix, but not evidence of anything flag-related. All counts below use `positions`/`trades` as ground truth, not raw `strategy_executions.action_taken` tallies.
2. **~20 stale `OPEN` position rows exist in staging's `positions` table from dead sessions** (sessions 1,2,3,4,5,7,8,9,12,14 — pre-dating this cohort by weeks), confirmed and excluded. Only **one** `OPEN` row belongs to the live session (id 39, session 20). Do not let a naive `count(*) WHERE status='OPEN'` (=20) stand in for "open positions" anywhere in this analysis.
3. **Session 19→20 boundary (2026-07-08 15:42) reassigns `session_id` on still-open positions, not open time.** Position/trade #38 (`entry_time` 2026-07-08 08:29:15, opened under session 19, which didn't end until 15:42:11) carries `session_id=20` because the reconciler re-associates a position that survives a restart with the new active session. Chronology in this report is built from `entry_time`/`exit_time`/`timestamp` columns, never from `session_id` grouping, to avoid misattributing activity across restarts.
4. Three staging engine restarts fell inside the window (`ENGINE_STOP`/`ENGINE_START` pairs at 07-06 12:23, 07-07 10:56, 07-08 15:42 — matching the 18→19 and 19→20 session boundaries). The 07-07 10:56 restart lines up with the `#929`/`#934` kill-switch-drill staging sync; the exact trigger for the 07-08 15:42 restart was not identified in `log.md` (most likely a routine develop-sync redeploy given the backlog of merges around that date) — flagged as a minor gap, not a concern.

**Honest caveat on comparability**: staging and prod are running the *same* strategy/symbol/code (develop-parity since 07-08), so this is a genuinely clean flags-on vs flags-off comparison at the code level. But the trade-count gap (3 closed trades vs 0) is **not** attributable to the cohort flags — prod's single open short has simply not hit its exit condition in 10 days, and staging happened to churn through several stop-loss exits in the same window. With n=3 realized trades on staging and n=0 on prod, this comparison cannot support any claim that the cohort flags improved (or hurt) trading outcomes — it only establishes that the flags did not produce anything alarming (no runaway sizing, no drawdown breach, no error storm).

## 4. Recommendations per flag

| Flag | Recommendation | Success criterion / horizon if EXTEND | risk_review_required |
|---|---|---|---|
| Regime Detection | **EXTEND** | Needs the detector to actually call `trend_down` at least once with plausible confidence during a real down-move before its labels can be trusted for anything downstream. Horizon: 30 days, or immediately upon any ETHUSDT drawdown week (>10% over 7 days), whichever first. | Not itself sizing-affecting (it only labels), but any promotion decision must bundle it with its consumers below — mark **true** at that time. |
| Exposure Governor | **EXTEND** | Needs one real `trend_down` or `high_vol` read with the governor's cap actually binding tighter than the 20% system cap (an `exposure_cap_reached_*`/`exposure_capped_*` tag observed), then a review of whether the resulting size reduction was sane. Same horizon as regime detection — it's gated on the same missing regime. | **true** — directly sizing/exposure-affecting; no promotion without risk-officer stress-testing a real cap-binding event. |
| Macro Event Guard | **EXTEND, blocked on GH #962 / PR #965** | Cannot begin a valid observation period until PR #965 (open, not merged) lands. Once fixed: observe a real `macro_event_block_*` tag around the next covered event (CPI Jul 14 is 2 days out — a good near-term test window) and confirm entries are blocked/reduced without side effects on exits (guard should never block exits/stops — verify this explicitly, #806 doesn't ask for it but it should be checked). | **true** — it changes entries/exposure around events; not ready for any promotion decision yet. |

| Circuit Breakers (dry_run) | **EXTEND, with a prerequisite fast-follow** | Wire `dry_run` trips to `system_events` (source trace shows this is a small, well-scoped change) so evidence survives container restarts — right now we cannot even confirm zero trips occurred over 98% of the cohort. After that lands: run a full ~2-week cohort and require either zero DB-recorded trips or a reviewed explanation for each one, before **any** consideration of `active` mode. | **true** — this is explicitly a capital-protection mechanism (#807: daily-loss halt + drawdown throttle); any move toward `active` needs full risk-officer stress-testing (drawdown scenarios per #807's own spec) before it can touch prod. |

| kelly_momentum paper trial | **DECIDE (not a flag; separate action item)** — provision the second staging vehicle or formally park the proposal with a written reason, within 1 week | N/A | false while it remains paper-only (matches the original 2026-07-04 risk-officer verdict: paper + staging = inside the charter autonomy envelope, zero capital at risk). |

**None of the above are ready for prod promotion.** The strongest honest finding this cohort produced is a *negative* one: the cohort ran for 6+ days without the tape ever presenting the bear/high-vol/macro-event conditions its own name promises to defend against, so nothing about its defensive value has actually been tested yet. The closest thing to a positive result — exposure governor and cross-symbol guard both being correctly inert when they should be inert — is real but modest: it shows the machinery doesn't misfire in benign conditions, not that it works when it matters.
